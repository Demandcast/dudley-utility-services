Life at DUS

What working here actually feels like

A job posting tells you the work you'll do. The part that decides whether you stay is everything around it. Here's a preview of life at Dudley.

Privately owned

Dudley has been privately owned since 1980. Decisions get made inside the company, for the long run.

Built over 45+ years

Dudley has spent 45+ years empowering leaders, earning trust at every position, and building integrity into the work.

Careers built deliberately

People have joined Dudley as students and recent grads and stayed long enough to become the leaders running it today.

We're all about doing the work the right way. It starts with hiring passionate people from a wide range of backgrounds, then building an environment where they have the support and trust to take on the country's biggest utility projects together.
