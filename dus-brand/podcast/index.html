PROGRESSIVE PARCELS

As episodes increase, more parcel cells fill green. EP 01 has 1 highlighted cell. EP 05 has 4. EP 12 has 10. This creates a visual narrative of progress that mirrors how a right-of-way project moves forward.

The highlighted parcel count should roughly track: one new cell per episode, adding left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

EPISODE TITLE RULES

Max 2 lines at 22px (preview scale). If a title runs longer, tighten the copy. Guest name is optional and uses a lighter weight to maintain hierarchy.

ZONES

Top bar: Podcast name and episode number. Fixed height, never overlaps content.

Parcel strip: Vertical (left edge) or horizontal (top edge) row of parcel cells. Acts as brand texture without interfering with content.

Content zone: Dark recessed area for video or audio waveform. Clear boundaries on all sides.

Bottom bar: Guest name, title, and episode title on a gradient backdrop. Stays readable over any content.

SAFE AREAS

The content zone has 12px minimum clearance from all edges. The bottom bar uses a gradient backdrop so text stays legible regardless of what's in the video frame above.

Max 3 lines at 20px (preview scale). Keep quotes tight. If a quote runs long, trim it to the strongest clause. The card works best with 15-30 words.

SYSTEM
Brand System Notes
Rules that govern all assets in this podcast identity.

SURFACE

All assets use the horizontal teal-to-green sweep (--pbp-sweep) or its vertical variant (--pbp-sweep-v). The survey grid overlay is always present. No flat black backgrounds, no light teal section fills.

PARCEL GRID

The signature visual element. Cells are 1px white border at 10% opacity. "Active" cells get 24% opacity border and a 3% white fill. "Highlighted" cells get green border and 8% green fill. Never exceed 2 highlighted cells on the main cover, and scale highlights to match episode count on episode art.

TYPOGRAPHY

Lato only. Weights: 900 (titles), 700 (labels, eyebrows, attribution names), 400 (body, subtitles, guest titles), 300 (secondary text like "A DUS PODCAST"). All display text is uppercase. No other typeface.

GREEN ACCENT

Green appears in: eyebrow labels, parcel highlights, CTA buttons, quotation marks, episode numbers. Combined green coverage stays under 8% of any composition. Never darken green to a variant.

CORNER TICKS

Present on every asset. 24px arms on the cover, 16px arms on smaller assets. White at 22% opacity on dark surfaces.

TEXT OVERLAP RULE

No text element shares vertical space with any other text element or the parcel grid. Each asset divides into clear zones: grid zone (upper), text zone (lower), with a minimum 16px gap between them. The clip frames add a third zone (content/video) that is walled off on all sides.

CONTRAST SCRIM

A teal-tinted gradient scrim (rgba(3,51,73,0.85) fading to transparent) sits behind every text zone. This ensures all text passes WCAG AA contrast against the green end of the sweep gradient, where raw white opacity alone would fail. The scrim is invisible on the dark teal end and only activates visually where the background lightens toward green.

EXPORT

All assets export via Chrome headless screenshot. Match --window-size to the preview dimensions and use --force-device-scale-factor to hit the target resolution. Survey grid renders clean as CSS repeating gradients (no rasterization artifacts).
